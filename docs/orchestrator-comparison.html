Three ways to build software with AI agents · 29 Sep 2026

Anthill, AutoDev and Factory, feature by feature

AutoDev is strongest at checking and cost. Factory is strongest at running on its own. Anthill is strongest at remembering, and at keeping many chats in step. None of them yet checks whether a custom feature's answers are actually right.

AutoDev

Finishes one ticket as cheaply as it can.

Sorts the ticket, plans, codes in a separate copy, checks, reviews. Counts every token. Runs on Claude or Codex.

Source: its own architecture document, Rev. 24

Factory v2

Builds an agreed feature list overnight, on its own.

Interview up front, then plan, build, and a fresh QA session that needs proof. Never asks mid-build.

Source: its own factory map, v2

Anthill

Speeds up the owner working with agents on custom logic.

Every chat starts knowing the work, the owner's decisions and the traps. Nothing closes without a passing check.

Source: its code, 98 tests passing

What only one of them has

Only Anthill

  • A map of the code that spots stale notes
  • One shared view of all work, across chats
  • What is being edited right now
  • A live page that starts with the app
  • Files agents may not edit
  • Owner signatures on notes

Only AutoDev

  • Sorting requests by size
  • Pulling work from a ticket tracker
  • Three kinds of question
  • Ask a person after two failed searches
  • Risky steps pause for the owner
  • Rejected options recorded

Only Factory

  • Fresh QA that starts from “broken”
  • Proof required for every promise
  • Clicking through the real app
  • Restarts itself after sleep
  • Waits out usage limits
  • Cost forecasts

None of them

  • A locked accuracy score for custom work. It would have caught a Kerala story pinned in Finland. Agreed as Anthill's next step after the buttons.
AutoDev and Factory are described from their own documents, not their code, so their entries are their own claims. Anthill is described from its code as of 29 September 2026.
